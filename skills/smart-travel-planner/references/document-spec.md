# Document specification
V1.0.2 presentation: render relative daily labels once; keep route coverage/engineering status in structured Plan and `developer-report.json`. The tourist handbook presents the readable sequence of known places and a centralized list of meaningful pending checks. Do not render unknown airport-to-unknown airport edges or grids of empty costs. Preserve all original unknown facts, sources, dates, numeric qualifiers and evidence in the source data. Known route references retain estimated/current/verified qualifications; never replace absent data with numbers for appearance.
Full multi-day plans default to DOCX, without asking again. Single travel questions do not. Use the bundled `deliver_plan.py` (or skill-local entry `deliver`), not a temporary generator. It diagnoses paths, dependencies, JSON, images and outputs, retries the standard builder once, and logs failure separately. All root scripts are located from the full installed plugin, independent of cwd; a detached skill degrades explicitly.

Lead with overview and personalized highlights, daily route, photo-and-prose experience introductions, seasonal topic, lodging, transport, meals, budget, alternatives, checklist, sources and important unknowns. Use tables for schedules, numbers and comparisons, not long descriptive cards. Currency codes are explicit: CNY 人民币 / JPY 日元; no unqualified yen-shaped symbols. Unknown symbols without an item currency are labeled currency unknown, never guessed from geography. Future unannounced events have conditional schedules and alternatives, no fixed booking date. Use actual attraction binding for images and include authors/licensing. Render if supported; otherwise structural PASS and visual UNVERIFIED stay distinct.
Create a polished, readable DOCX with cover, summary, trip profile, destination, budget table, lodging areas, transport, sights, daily itinerary, food, savings, weather Plan B, reservations, risks, checklist, and evidence/check times. Use python-docx, tables for structured comparisons, hyperlinks for evidence, and accessible heading hierarchy. Validate archive and non-empty content. Include image captions and license/source.

Do not build a final DOCX until `trip_readiness` passes: confirmed city/area, travel window, duration, party size, origin, budget amount/currency/scope and airfare treatment; if complimentary lodging is planned, its location and dates/nights. Internal `EvidenceRecord` claims and tool payloads stay out of the user document. Render only a curated public source index (title, URL, checked time). Use a static clickable table of contents with internal anchors (no Word update placeholder), page numbering, deliberate page breaks, repeatable table headers, and bounded images. Captions include source/attribution and license; unknown-license images are omitted. Keep technical terminology and acceptance/test content out of user output.
