# Questioning strategy
For vague wishes, use `experience-discovery.md` after initial research. Ask about interest depth and meaningful priority differences, not only dates/count. Use the existing three-subjective-decision budget and at most five questions per round. “You decide” ends repeated preference asks. Backfill answers into experience_preferences and use them to change candidate selection. Available cash is not automatically a travel budget; keep currency and budget purpose pending. Do not infer a chosen city or official future festival date from the user's broad intent.
Apply the Ask Value Test from `information-classification.md`; unknown facts alone do not justify a question. Research first, then ask at most five concise questions. `USER_REQUIRED` facts include origin, dates/flexibility, party size, budget/currency/scope, and hard personal constraints. Optional preferences such as pace/interests can be skipped with an explicit assumption. Do not ask the user to supply agent-discoverable hotel, neighborhood, airport, flight, attraction, transit, ticket, route, or restaurant knowledge. Present researched trade-offs with a recommended default before asking `USER_DECISION` questions. When the user delegates, use `AUTO_DECISION_MODE`; do not ask again for delegated choices. Never treat delegation as permission to book/pay.
